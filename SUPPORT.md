# Getting help

- **Read first:** the [documentation](https://docs.anetos.dev), its
  [Getting started](https://docs.anetos.dev/getting-started/) and its
  [glossary](https://docs.anetos.dev/concepts/glossary/). Most guides
  have a "Common problems" section.
- **Ask:** in [Discussions](https://github.com/anetos-dev/anetos/discussions),
  in the Q&A category. Say what you're trying to do, what you tried, and
  what happened; include `go version`, the Anetos version (`go list -m
  anetos.dev/anetos`) and the database. Code and errors as text, not
  screenshots.
- **Report a bug or propose a change:** an
  [issue](https://github.com/anetos-dev/anetos/issues/new/choose).
- **A security vulnerability:** privately, never in public:
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Anetos is maintained by volunteers: answers come when they can.
